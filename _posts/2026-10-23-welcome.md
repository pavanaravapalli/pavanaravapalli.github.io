I’m **Pavan**, a Cloud Infrastructure Product Architect with 14 years of experience building and scaling enterprise cloud platforms.

My work spans the full stack—from low-level hypervisors (_**KVM, XenServer, VMware**_) and open-source orchestration (Apache CloudStack) to cloud federation and microservices architecture. Over the years, I’ve architected zero-downtime enterprise private clouds like **Rovius CloudPlatform** (formerly **Citrix CloudPlatform**) and hybrid cloud brokerage platforms like the **Cloud Ecosystem Hub**.

## What I write about here:

### Private Cloud Architecture: 
    Design patterns for zero-downtime upgrades, multi-cloud federation, 
    and resource orchestration.

### Hypervisor & Operations Notes: 
    Field lessons and troubleshooting from running KVM, XenServer,
    and VMware in production.


### Apache CloudStack & Open Source:
    Deep dives, performance tuning, and practical notes 
    for cloud operators.
